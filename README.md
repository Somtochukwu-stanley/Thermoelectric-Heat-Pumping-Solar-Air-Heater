# Thermoelectric-Heat-Pumping-Solar-Air-Heater
Design, fabrication, and performance evaluation of a thermoelectric heat-pumping solar air heater integrating 11 thermoelectric modules to improve thermal efficiency and heat transfer performance

# Project Highlights
* Designed, fabricated, and experimentally evaluated a thermoelectric heat-pumping solar air heater.
* Integrated 11 thermoelectric modules to enhance thermal performance.
* Achieved a 31.5% improvement in thermal efficiency compared to a conventional solar air heater.
* Increased average thermal efficiency from 19% to 25%.
* Recorded a peak efficiency of 74%, compared to 57% for the conventional system.
* Improved outlet air temperature by approximately 4–6°C

# Overview
This project focused on the development of a thermoelectric heat-pumping solar air heater aimed at improving the performance of conventional solar air heating systems. The system combines solar energy with thermoelectric heat-pumping technology to enhance heat transfer, increase outlet air temperature, and improve overall thermal efficiency.
The project involved system design, prototype fabrication, experimental testing, data collection, performance evaluation, and comparison with a conventional solar air heater.

# Objectives
* Design and fabricate a thermoelectric heat-pumping solar air heater.
* Improve the thermal efficiency of a conventional solar air heater.
* Experimentally evaluate the performance of the developed system.
* Compare the thermal performance of the proposed design with a conventional solar air heater.

# Methodology
## Design and Fabrication

The solar air heater was designed and fabricated with integrated thermoelectric modules to improve heat transfer performance. The system was constructed and assembled for experimental evaluation under outdoor operating conditions.

## Experimental Testing

Performance testing was conducted by monitoring:

* Solar radiation intensity
* Inlet air temperature
* Outlet air temperature
* Thermal efficiency
The developed system was tested and compared with a conventional solar air heater to evaluate performance improvements.
